**Subject:** Request for a photograph — Nikolaj JANOK (JANAK), born 28.08.1924, Weprik — I.T.S. FOTO No. 1341

Dear Arolsen Archives team,

I am researching the history of the village of Vepryk (Weprik), Chernihiv region, Ukraine, for a family history project. In your online collection I found records of

- **Nikolaj JANOK (JANAK)**, born **28.08.1924** in Weprik, Kr. Czernikow (Ukraine);
- Concentration camp Stutthof, prisoner No. **15680**, arrived 10.08.1942; transferred to KL Dachau on 14.11.1942;
- a later record from Buchenwald, prisoner No. **46252**, with the note **«I.T.S. FOTO No. 1341»**.
- Online references: https://collections.arolsen-archives.org/en/search/person/4502354

Could you please let me know whether the photograph «I.T.S. FOTO No. 1341» is preserved in your archive and send me a digital copy (or tell me how to order it)? Any further documents about his fate after 1945 would also be very welcome.

The person is probably not a direct relative of mine; I would like to pass the photograph and the documents on to people from his village who may be his relatives.

Thank you very much for your help.
