Why Turkish tyres, why now

Turkish tyres become the best value in Ukraine from 2027

With duty falling from 10% to 0%, Turkish agricultural tyres land in Ukraine at a lower average customs value per kilogram than Indian or Chinese tyres that still pay 10%. Delivery by truck takes about a week instead of several weeks by sea.

Demand is broad: the large fleet of older MTZ and YuMZ tractors, backhoe loaders, seeders, telehandlers, skid steers and forklifts — plus modern radial sizes for large agricultural holdings.
